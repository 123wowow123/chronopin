# Electricity generation projects — cost-qualified collection

Published **37 verified project pins across 18 country/area labels**, including **17 U.S. projects**. The live global and U.S. maps matched the eligible catalog exactly. Content was saved through production management APIs; no production VM deploy, build or restart was performed.

Browse the [worldwide map](https://www.chronopin.com/map?q=tag%3A%22Electric%20Generation%20Projects%22) or [U.S. map](https://www.chronopin.com/map?q=tag%3A%22Electric%20Generation%20Projects%22%20place%3A%22United%20States%22).

## Final inclusion rule

Only documented physical construction, construction complete/commissioning, partial operation while the rest is being built, or recent **2025–2026** project openings qualify. Every retained pin has a **published project-specific construction/capital investment figure** and **published electrical generating capacity**. Announcements, permits, equipment orders, PPAs and financing alone do not establish physical construction. Low-confidence proposals and unbuilt future expansions are excluded.

No construction costs were estimated from technology benchmarks. Loan amounts, acquisition prices, company/portfolio investment totals, economic benefits and operating revenues are not used as project costs. Published project spending components are summed only where explicitly identified: Cumberland, Kingston and Allen. These calculated sums are labelled on the pins; Allen is an upper spending forecast, not a fixed-price quote.

The output field is MW generating capacity, with AC/DC, net/nameplate and rounded/operator conventions preserved. It is not actual annual energy production. Expected annual GWh, where published, are described as forecasts. Storage capacity and transmission ratings are not added to generating MW. Original cost scopes and currencies are preserved; there is no converted global total or implied uniform price year.

## Important scope and date distinctions

- **SunZia:** one 3,650 MW whole-project pin; USD11 billion includes wind and transmission. North/South inventory views are not separate projects with duplicate budgets. Transmission capacity is not generation.
- **Mammoth:** original 1,300 MW complex and historical 2022 USD1.5 billion estimate, including the older 400 MW North phase. Its 900 MWac final phases are completing/commissioning in October 2026. The full budget is not described as the cost of those 900 MW alone, and the proposed 2,245 MW expansion is excluded.
- **Obelisk, MTerra and Redstone:** published project cost includes integrated battery or thermal-storage infrastructure. Storage MW/MWh/hours are separately labelled. MTerra’s first 600 MWac contracted mid-merit supply does not mean the entire 3,500 MWp PV complex is operational.
- **Darlington:** 300 MWe Unit 1 plus shared-site infrastructure budget CAD7.7 billion. Three future reactors and the CAD20.9 billion fleet budget are excluded.
- **Hinkley Point C:** EDF’s GBP35 billion completion estimate is in **2015 sterling**, not nominal 2026 money. Both reactors are under construction; later target dates do not make them proposal-only projects.
- **Empire Wind 1:** USD5 billion includes terminal-use fees and expected investment tax credits. It is a tax-credit-adjusted capital estimate, distinct from gross expenditure or project financing.
- **Natrium, Orange County, Sofia and Magnolia:** their published estimates are explicitly dated; the research does not imply a refreshed 2026 final construction budget. Natrium’s DOE contribution is separate from total project cost.
- **Johnsonville:** TVA’s updated USD652 million project budget and 530 MW summer net output cover only new units 21–30; old units are excluded. Older USD619 million figures are superseded.
- **Legend/Lone Star:** September 2025 budgets supersede older proposal costs, and April 2026 groundbreaking supplies physical evidence. Economic-impact figures are excluded.
- **Mill Creek 5:** the operator’s current 645 MW nameplate and USD902 million cost are used. Older 640 MW and EIA’s 740 MW inventory rating are not extra capacity.
- **Globeleq Menengai:** only its 35 MW plant and USD117 million later project investment are included, not the inventory’s broader 70 MW grouping or other developers’ plants.
- **Shuaibah:** one pin for the two completed solar plants, 2,660 MWac and the published USD2.37 billion combined cost. Individual phases do not repeat this total.
- **Lower Yarlung Zangbo:** actual July 2025 construction launch; CNY1.2 trillion is the whole five-station programme investment. The 60,000 MW design figure comes from GEM, not the government cost announcement. The point is an approximate project location, not five surveyed dam entrances.
- **First fire, first power, inauguration and commercial operation differ:** Orange County first fire, CVOW/Baltic/Hai Long partial generation and He Dreiht inauguration are not represented as full commercial completion. Historical targets are not proof that a plant opened.

Timeline dates are actual dated milestones where available, or clearly estimated reporting/period-end markers. A month/quarter-only date does not invent an exact commercial-operation day. The operator evidence may supersede older inventory status, especially Delta Blues, Allen, Dzhankeldy and Mammoth.

## Research coverage and limits

This is a **verified subset, not an exhaustive census of every qualifying project worldwide**. Initial inventory screening inspected 182,593 GEM unit/phase rows and prepared 33,184 project-level candidate records before overlap reconciliation. The tighter construction-only staging had 11,017 new project records before the cost rule. These large inventory counts are research candidates, not published cost-qualified projects. Their individual budgets have not all been audited; an omitted project may have a published cost that this pass has not verified.

The U.S. work used the [EIA August 2026 generator inventory](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/xls/august_generator2026.xlsx), 37 initial detailed operator/regulator updates, additional project-level cost research, SEC filings, government investment releases and dated construction/commissioning announcements. Seventeen U.S. projects passed the final paired cost/output/physical-work checks. [Detailed U.S. research and exclusions](electric-generation-usa-research-2026-10-11.json) retains reasons for gaps rather than inventing costs. Yates’ inspected public budget was redacted; financing did not substitute for costs at Wharton, Palisades, Cape or Hermes 2. Recent backup/standby units without verified budgets are omitted.

Worldwide coordinates and inventory context use [GEM’s inspected August 2026 release](https://github.com/GlobalEnergyMonitor/gipt-dashboard/blob/main/Global%20Integrated%20Power%20August%202026.xlsx), with [CC BY 4.0 attribution](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Older coordinate context is used for Bash, Dzhankeldy and Redstone; their operator reports establish current project status. Plant points may be approximate. Workbook URL fragments are logical project identifiers, not spreadsheet row-jump controls. August snapshots and dated operator updates do not imply a fresh October site inspection everywhere.

The eligible catalog is [machine-readable JSON Lines](electric-generation-pins-2026-10-11.jsonl). Wider proposal research is kept in ignored local checkpoints and is absent from the map and eligible catalog. 6,228 removed task-created import IDs were verified hidden, including proposal, no-cost and duplicate-phase records. Earlier historical pins were preserved with the collection tag removed when they failed the final rule.

## Photos and gallery checks

Official project pages and linked galleries/photo pages were checked. 59 page/gallery fetch records are preserved in local research checkpoints. Eight retained/reviewed project images are stored in publicly verified Azure blobs with original image URLs, operator/photographer credits and accurate captions. These cover CCSM, Mill Creek, Empire Wind, Aldoga, Horizonte and He Dreiht. Mill Creek’s rendering is labelled an illustration; Horizonte’s image is labelled an inauguration event with turbines in the background. Photo files were not added to Git.

Gallery gaps remain: OPG returned 403, Baltic Power had a direct connection failure, RWE’s linked Canto gallery returned a JavaScript shell, and EnBW’s Bynder gallery required an interactive download flow. EnBW’s directly linked, credited project photograph was accessible and verified. Pages containing only logos, generic corporate media or unrelated projects were not used as project photos. No enabled browser was available for interactive gallery/UI inspection. Many retained pins still have no newly verified representative photo.
