* **Update**: **The timeline's pick now weighs a pin's evidence.** A crowded day shows two rows and picks them at random by weight, and until now that weight read only what the card earned (opens and watches per impression) and the money on any markets it cited. Two more terms join it ([bagSample.ts](../../src/lib/bagSample.ts)): **how well the pin's own sources back it**, on a curve drawn across the band the timeline actually shows - `TIMELINE_MIN_CONFIDENCE` (70) counts for x1 and 100 for x1.6, falling on the same slope to a floor of x0.5 below the threshold rather than off a cliff, with an unscored pin left at x1 because "pins with no score at all still show" - and **how well its thread is sourced**, the mean confidence of the *other* pins in its chain, on the same curve at a third of the strength (a perfect thread is x1.2). A chain corroborates a pin without being its own evidence, and the pin's own confidence is left out of its thread's because it is already counted. It is a sampling weight, never a filter: nothing is hidden by it. The pin's own confidence is computed on the card from references it already carries, so the server and the hydrating client still pick the same cards; `threadConfidence` cannot be - the rest of a thread is rarely on the page - so the timeline query sends it, seeded by the page's own ids so the cost follows the page and not the corpus (a recursive walk both ways over `IX_Pin_parentId`, 3.6ms for 40 seeds, leaving `/api/main` at ~19ms). [Enrichment](/scraping/enrichment.md) gains a section on all four terms, because a scrape's references now decide how often its pin is the card a busy day shows.
