Production data now covers 35 pins, including five additional resolved candidates: US midterms (1648), Kenya (2360), El Salvador (2352), Latvia (5171) and Guatemala (2358). All five were saved through the authenticated admin API, read back against the prepared geometry, and checked on their public pages. Their authors, titles, descriptions, summaries, dates, locations, source URLs and parents remain unchanged. The expanded audit records 12 remaining source gaps; coverage is not complete for those pins.

## US plan comparisons

Pin 1648 stores ten comparisons: Alabama (7 districts), California (52), Florida (28), Louisiana (6), Missouri (8), North Carolina (14), Ohio (15), Tennessee (9), Texas (38) and Utah (4). Each contains an earlier and later plan: 362 alternate-plan features total. Controls select a state and show earlier, later or both, with separate color/dash styles, plan vintage, status and linked source. All control labels are translated in the 16 supported languages.

Missouri’s later map is explicitly **blocked/on hold**, and its earlier 2022 plan is the default for the November election. A comparison is not evidence that a later plan is operative. The source catalog is [us-redraw-sources-2026-10-25.json](../../../scripts/boundaries/us-redraw-sources-2026-10-25.json), with the exact government downloads and status links. [NCSL’s changing-maps tracker](https://www.ncsl.org/redistricting-and-census/changing-the-maps-tracking-mid-decade-redistricting?maptype=tile) and the [AP map reference guide](https://developer.ap.org/ap-elections-api/mapfiles/MapFiles_RefGuide.htm) were checked for state-by-state changes and Missouri’s reversal.

The [Census 119th-Congress layer](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Legislative/MapServer/4) supplies the earlier plans. The [120th-Congress layer](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Legislative/MapServer/0) supplies the nationwide base. Its service description references January 2026, but the fetched geometry contains subsequent state changes: metadata alone was not treated as a reliable geometry cutoff. Alabama, California, Florida and Louisiana use the original government shapefiles instead; Missouri is restored from the earlier plan for the default. The remaining states retain Census geometry with explicit adoption/status citations. Both national snapshots contain 435 unique voting-district GEOIDs; territories, DC and unassigned water codes are excluded.

Official state geometry is validated before conversion, simplified in EPSG:5070 at 100 metres while preserving topology, then converted to WGS84. Stored coordinates are not modified for Alaska: only map display longitudes wrap near the pin to avoid the antimeridian producing an unusable frame. Changing earlier/later/compare preserves the viewport; changing state fits the union of both plans.

## Four additional resolved countries

- **Kenya:** [IEBC’s archive distributed by OCHA HDX](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/kenya-elections) supplies exactly 290 named constituency IDs, 1–290. Five additional unnamed code-zero pieces have nonzero area but no district assignment and are excluded with an explicit map annotation and preparation report. No invalid polygon is repaired silently. [IEBC’s January 2026 statement](https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/jmFjJrsim3.pdf) establishes no boundary review before 2027. This supersedes the rejected hosted mirror, whose identifiers and rings were malformed. Senate contests are countywide and are distinguished from the drawn Assembly constituencies.
- **El Salvador:** [geoBoundaries ADM1](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/SLV/ADM1/) supplies 14 departmental outlines (2021 geometry, built 2023; OpenStreetMap, CC BY-SA 2.0). These are labelled as a geographic overview of the 14 legislative constituencies, with no claim to official 2027 polling or municipal geometry. The direct government download attempts failed; provenance and vintage remain visible.
- **Latvia:** [VARAM/State Land Service 2026 administrative geometry](https://data.gov.lv/dati/dataset/atr) (CC0) is aggregated from all 42 source units into exactly five Saeima constituencies using [Saeima Election Law section 7(2)](https://m.likumi.lv/ta/id/35261), current August–December 2026 edition. Every administrative unit is assigned once. Overseas votes count in Riga. Source CRS EPSG:3059 is transformed to WGS84 after 75-metre topology-preserving generalisation.
- **Guatemala:** [CONRED/OCHA HDX](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-gtm), CC BY 3.0 IGO, supplies department and municipality geometry valid 2019-02-07. The capital municipality GT0101 is separated exactly from department GT01, producing 23 districts rather than incorrectly drawing only 22 departments. The country union is checked before generalisation; national-list seats remain nationwide. The original polygons are valid and the capital has no area outside its parent department.

## Reproduction and evidence

Use `scripts/boundaries/fetch-us-redraws.mjs`, then `scripts/boundaries/prepare-us-redraws.py` with pyshp, pyproj and shapely installed in an ignored temporary environment. It validates district counts and identifiers. Source geometry remains under ignored `.scrape/boundaries/`, never in Git. Recheck legal status before replaying; these are reviewed snapshots, not an automatic live-map feed.

Country transformations are in `prepare-kenya-2012.py`, `prepare-latvia-2026.py` and `prepare-guatemala.py`. Replay entries are in `backfill-redraws-2026-10-25.prod.json` and `backfill-resolved-2026-10-25.prod.json`; the complete 35-pin list is in `backfill-2026-10-25.prod.json`. The importer validates nested comparison geometry/status/source links and preserves per-feature attribution when requested. Existing non-district layers are retained.

Ignored verification evidence includes `new-boundaries-verified.json` (read-back hashes, original pin-field comparisons and five public-page checks), `redraw-ui-verification.json` (browser toggles and path/color counts), screenshots, raw government downloads and pre-write rollback snapshots. Focused unit tests cover layer selection, default isolation, antimeridian nonmutation, plan validation, accessible controls and translations; TypeScript checks pass.
