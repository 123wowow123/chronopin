- Piombino's revised EUR 3.2 billion and possible spring-2027 start supersede earlier estimates; permitting progress is not physical construction.

- Warrenton's USD 85 billion is a bond ceiling for acquisition/refresh financing, not campus construction cost. Early-2027 equipment acquisition remains a forecast.
- ORD-03 uses 520,000 square feet per building, two buildings and 21 planned jobs. Its historical proposal does not establish current physical work.

- Mailuu-Suu records the EUR 23 million grant and seven-year remediation scope, distinct from later programme-wide funding. A grant is not proof that remediation finished.
- Renovation and upgrade awards with undisclosed customer sites retain the published project scope without inventing a plant identity or allocated generation capacity.
- Existing numeric pin prices were preserved. Values not established as current project capex by this batch remain separate research gaps, including financing/loan values, combined-project spending and extraction anomalies.
