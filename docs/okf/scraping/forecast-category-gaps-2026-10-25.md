# Forecast category gap research — October 25, 2026

This pass adds 16 panels and supplies matches for 19 previously uncovered fixed
categories. The synchronized catalog now contains 50 panels matching 58 of 59
fixed categories. Category matching means relevant context is available; it does
not mean every panel forecasts the whole category or every region. Art remains a
survey outlook rather than a numerical market-size projection. The original 31
panels and three subsequently added Ramp token panels are preserved.

All sources below were opened over HTTPS. Verbatim evidence, units, source dates
(or null when the publication day is unsupported) and scope notes are stored in
`scripts/industry/forecasts.json`. No exchange-rate conversions, invented annual
points, midpoint estimates or candidate probabilities derived from betting odds
were used. NASA's monthly values were read directly from its linked text table.

## Remaining gap and rejected substitutions

- **Award:** Checked Academy/oscars.org searches for 2027 predictions and early
  awards coverage. The Academy supplies schedules and eligibility information,
  not numerical win probabilities. The AP early-predictions article and other
  rankings do not supply a verified numerical forecasting model. Do not turn an
  ordinal ranking, critic preference or award calendar into a probability, or
  add the movie industry's total revenue merely to make this category match.
  Next useful research is a named award/category/candidate with a documented
  forecast or explicitly identified prediction-market snapshot and observation
  date. No generic Awards panel is fabricated.
- **Justice alternative:** The Ministry of Justice's December 2025 prison
  projections and January 2026 capacity statement use differing assumptions and
  figures. They were checked, along with later parliamentary/IfG material; do
  not treat prison capacity, an older central population projection and a
  sentencing-reform scenario as interchangeable. The current UN homicide
  projection supplies this category's panel instead.
- **Religion alternative:** The 2015/2017 worldwide projections are older than
  the 2022 US scenario and do not incorporate newer observed US switching
  evidence. They are not relabeled as a current global forecast.
- **Access limits:** The 2025 UNDRR HLPF-input PDF returned HTTP 403. The
  accessible UNDRR source and opened UNDP article support the disaster row;
  no evidence depends on the inaccessible PDF. Commercial full reports remain
  paid; their public abstracts support the stated figures but cannot establish
  agreement among competing methodologies.
