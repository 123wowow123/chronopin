Researched October 9, 2026. The `/products?shelf=alcoholic-drinks` section
uses the `Alcoholic Drinks` topic tag and the existing current-year product
query. Empty shelves stay hidden. Cards include the release date and distinguish
released products from upcoming products. Ordinary Food/Retail categories remain
on the pins, with Beer, Vodka or Ready-to-drink Cocktails as topic tags.

The checked pin bodies, source references, producer photo credits, public Azure
blob URLs and SHA-256 checksums are in [the catalog](product-drinks-2026.json).
All seven new photos were downloaded into ignored `.tmp/drinks`, checked visually,
uploaded to Azure and downloaded again to verify their hashes before pinning.

The two additional Goose Island pins cover individual products, rather than

preorders corroborate November 27; the original lineup was also checked against
July 21 reporting from Forbes and Beeronaut.

Absolut's global announcement gives a February rollout, while the U.S.
announcement says available January 28. This pin explicitly tracks the U.S.
launch. Carling's pin covers the new U.K. formulation, not longstanding Black
Label products in other countries. High Noon is a limited allocation; its
historical launch date does not establish current retailer stock.

Only Berry Rouge's announcement publishes a suggested retail price ($25.99,
750 mL, USD). Other entries state that the producer did not publish a price.
No review ratings or current stock claims were invented. Searches also checked
Molson Coors' 2026 innovation announcements, BrewDog's January small batches,
Gray Whale's vodka announcement and Bourbon County retailer preorders.
Nonalcoholic launches and 2027 products were excluded from this section.
