## Research and replenishment

Continue each city's opening research using branch operator announcements and current local reporting. Just opened requires verified actual or eligible recent opening evidence; do not turn a past estimated date into a confirmed opening. Top restaurants require supported venue content and recognition where claimed; do not fabricate review scores. Specials require verified active/upcoming terms and local schedules, with published offer items and prices when available. Expired offers cannot fill a shortage.

The [initial research notes](restaurant-japan-expansion-2026-10-13.md) list checked operator sites, menus, galleries and reporting, with specific unresolved gaps for SMAAK Hakata, Shunka Sapporo, the museum pop-up and YEBISU BEER HALL TOKYO.
