# Taiwan restaurant guides

Researched October 10, 2026. Taipei, Taichung and Kaohsiung use the shared restaurant guide’s Coming soon, Just opened, Top restaurants and Specials views. Empty tabs are hidden and links to empty views fall back to populated content. City navigation runs Kaohsiung → Taichung → Taipei by longitude; Taiwan follows Australia and precedes Japan by westernmost supported city. All three cities use Asia/Taipei.

## Sources and remaining coverage gaps

Checked the operator information/menu/reservation pages and linked booking coverage. Dishes are deliberately not announced in advance, so the card lists the published tasting menu without inventing courses. Direct operator retrieval was intermittently unavailable; indexed operator text was accessible.
  Photo follow-up: the [official gallery](https://mume.tw/gallery.html) was successfully retrieved after the user identified this missed source. All seven MUME food photos were downloaded, visually inspected, converted to WebP, uploaded to Azure and verified publicly by SHA-256 and decoding. Photo 02 is the guide-card image; all seven are catalogued for the pin gallery with original source URLs and MUME credit. Unpublished dish names and current-menu eligibility are not inferred from the photos. Future pin research must check official galleries and linked photo pages, as recorded in AGENTS.md.
- **Smith & Wollensky:** [Taipei menu](https://www.smithandwollensky.com.tw/en/menu/taipei), [Taichung menu](https://www.smithandwollensky.com.tw/en/menu/taichung) and [operator branch directory](https://www.smithandwollensky.com.tw/en/locations). Checked HTML menus, linked fall/winter PDF menus, homepage and news. Food and drink happy-hour prices are separately transcribed for each branch; no cross-branch pricing. Both promotions exclude public holidays and festivals. Taiwan public holidays use the TW calendar supplemented with restored statutory holidays, Saturday substitutes and the day before Lunar New Year’s Eve, verified against the [2026 announcement](https://www.dgpa.gov.tw/information?pid=12574&uid=82) and [2027 calendar / statutory rules](https://www.dgpa.gov.tw/information?fid=9898&pid=12982&uid=30); additional operator-designated festival dates remain subject to branch confirmation. No offer expiry was published. Taichung’s complimentary snacks require an eligible drink and vary daily.
