# Taiwan restaurant guides

Researched October 10, 2026. Taipei, Taichung and Kaohsiung use the shared restaurant guide’s Coming soon, Just opened, Top restaurants and Specials views. Empty tabs are hidden and links to empty views fall back to populated content. City navigation runs Kaohsiung → Taichung → Taipei by longitude; Taiwan follows Australia and precedes Japan by westernmost supported city. All three cities use Asia/Taipei.

The catalog contains 12 pins and three scheduled special venues. “Top” means a Chronopin editorial selection, not a fabricated rating or award. TWD prices are transcribed from branch-specific sources; unpublished prices are labelled explicitly.

## Sources and remaining coverage gaps

- **MUME:** [operator reservation policy](https://mume.tw/reservation) publishes a NT$3,980 seasonal tasting menu plus 10%, excluding beverages. Checked the operator information/menu/reservation pages and linked booking coverage. Dishes are deliberately not announced in advance, so the card lists the published tasting menu without inventing courses. Direct operator retrieval was intermittently unavailable; indexed operator text was accessible.
- **Smith & Wollensky:** [Taipei menu](https://www.smithandwollensky.com.tw/en/menu/taipei), [Taichung menu](https://www.smithandwollensky.com.tw/en/menu/taichung) and [operator branch directory](https://www.smithandwollensky.com.tw/en/locations). Checked HTML menus, linked fall/winter PDF menus, homepage and news. Food and drink happy-hour prices are separately transcribed for each branch; no cross-branch pricing. Both promotions exclude public holidays and festivals. Taiwan public holidays use the TW calendar supplemented with restored statutory holidays, Saturday substitutes and the day before Lunar New Year’s Eve, verified against the [2026 announcement](https://www.dgpa.gov.tw/information?pid=12574&uid=82) and [2027 calendar / statutory rules](https://www.dgpa.gov.tw/information?fid=9898&pid=12982&uid=30); additional operator-designated festival dates remain subject to branch confirmation. No offer expiry was published. Taichung’s complimentary snacks require an eligible drink and vary daily.
- **Rough Cuts:** [IHG dining directory](https://www.ihg.com/intercontinental/hotels/us/en/taipei/tpepi/hoteldetail/dining), [IHG hotel address](https://www.ihg.com/intercontinental/hotels/gb/en/taipei/tpepi/hoteldetail), [SETN opening report via Mirror Media](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/external/setn_1919247) and [branch-specific October 8 menu reporting](https://taipeipost.org/397348/). No full priced menu verified.

## Media and locations

Six original source images were converted to WebP, uploaded to Azure Blob Storage and verified through public downloads with matching SHA-256 and successful decoding. The Smith & Wollensky image is explicitly labelled as a brand food photograph rather than either branch’s dining room. Blob/source URLs, credits and captions are in the catalogs; image binaries remain in ignored `.scrape/taiwan-guides/`.

Street addresses were matched using Esri World Geocoding, with candidate scores 98.42–100. Coordinates are approximate street-address positions, not surveyed entrances. Restaurants sharing a hotel share its street-address position. Menu profiles retain geocoding source URLs.
