## Price, media and location limits

The four priced menus explicitly identify Apple Maps/Dianping community recommendation data, fetched today, rather than guaranteed operator prices. None states portion or purchase conditions unless the menu note records it. Fetching the actual pages revealed stale search-index excerpts; only the fetched values are saved. Snapshots record the source URL, currency, checked date and conditions. The other six menus contain actual dishes even though prices are unavailable.

Dianping official-photo endpoints were checked for the nine identified branches; all returned HTTP 403. Their menu-image category pages returned identity-verification screens. Accessible, independently matched contributor galleries are used instead. Geng Fu Xing uses its branch-linked Trip.com gallery; no photos are borrowed from Wuhu. Dedicated authenticated operator galleries and complete current menu/ordering sheets remain follow-up gaps. Source captions, credits and original URLs accompany every retained image. Photos do not independently prove present dish availability. Every Azure blob was downloaded, SHA-256 verified and decoded before inclusion. Original downloads, contact sheets and WebP derivatives remain in ignored `.scrape/china-restaurants-round-three`, never in Git.

Nine branch positions come from the Ctrip page’s explicit `gaodeCoord` field, converted from GCJ-02 to WGS84. Shen He uses the Apple Maps/Dianping position, with GCJ-02 inferred from exact matches between that provider and Amap at three other researched branches; this datum uncertainty is recorded. All map locations are approximate and diners should confirm the entrance from the street address. Coarse locality geocoding results were rejected. City and country navigation remain west to east.
