## Menu and gallery research

All 60 Chinese dish names were matched against freshly fetched branch dish lists before import. Selections are real published dishes, not complete operator menus. Branch-specific operator, linked menu/PDF, ordering, price-table image and promotion searches went beyond the first listing. No current authenticated item prices were recovered; every dish and the reward explicitly says **“Price not published.”** Historical visitor prices, per-person averages, masked app prices and other-branch prices were excluded. No regular-menu dish is assumed to qualify for a promotion.

Seven included branches had usable Dianping visitor galleries. Their official-photo routes returned HTTP 403, and both menu-image and exact price-table categories returned identity verification rather than readable menu photographs. Visitor-gallery contributors retain their names and are identified as visitors. Ctrip original URLs supplied larger branch-gallery images; the listing did not identify those photographers, so credits explicitly say that. Food pictures do not independently prove a dish name or current availability. Near-duplicate congee and redundant entrance photographs were excluded after visual inspection. All selected images were inspected, converted to WebP, uploaded to Azure and checked through their public URL for matching SHA-256 bytes and decoding before DB references were saved. No photo or thumbnail is stored in Git.
