## Research and rejected candidates

- Branch suffixes confused Yelp search geography: “Crown Point” returned Indiana results, and “Mission Hills” returned Los Angeles results despite a San Diego location parameter. Searching the brand near its exact street address resolved Tajima Crown Point and La Puerta Mission Hills.
- [Chef JUN’s operator site](https://chefjun.com/) distinguishes Bay Ho, 4033 Avati Drive, from Point Loma, 3309 Rosecrans Street. Its Bay Ho booking link led to the verified Bay Ho Yelp branch and the accepted lunch-sashimi photograph. Point Loma photographs were excluded.
- Targeted searches recovered the five distinct BJ’s branch guacamole photographs, all showing chips, guacamole and salsa. Other recoveries included Gravity Heights Mission Valley tinga tacos, Portside Pier clam chowder, Tavern’s explicitly named mini portions, AVANT rolls, Sura takeout bento, and the three Tajima Crown Point food items.
- Eureka’s first labelled result was a sidewalk sign, not the dish. It was replaced before saving by a clearly labelled burger-and-fries photograph.
- The Search Bar’s tri-tip photograph showed two tacos, while the saved item specifies three; it was rejected. Its Yelp street name also differs from the stored profile and needs checking.
- Oggi’s named fish-and-chips photo showed three pieces for a two-piece lunch item; it was rejected. The photographed Stix did not establish the required half order.
- Other rejected matches included a shrimp-and-crab salad for “House salad,” a Smokehouse Burger for a plain burger special, topped tots variants, multi-dish table shots, and an explicitly uncertain cheese-board caption.
- Where no unit count is specified on the saved item, captions clarify multiple pictured units rather than implying that the photographed group is one priced unit.
