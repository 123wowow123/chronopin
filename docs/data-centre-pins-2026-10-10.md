## Dates, locations and coverage limits

Dates describe the specific milestone named on each card. Google Hamina and Saint-Ghislain use year-end boundaries for operator-published opening years; SV18 uses the end of its published opening quarter. Equinix SG6 and LD14 use March 31, 2027 as the boundary of their expected first-quarter opening window. Meta El Paso uses December 31, 2028 for the announced year in which capacity is expected to begin coming online. These boundaries are marked estimated and are not exact opening days.

Other pins use dated opening, groundbreaking, expansion, announcement or planning-review milestones. Planned facilities without published delivery windows do not receive invented future completion dates. Lea County remains exploratory, Jurong Island’s capacity allocation is provisional, and Pine Island’s city page states that required state permits are being pursued. De Soto remains a phased proposal tied to demand, infrastructure and future approvals.

Map markers are explicitly qualified. HK6, SV18 and LD14 use published street addresses; MD5 uses the regional government’s inauguration address (other campus sources use a different street number). De Soto uses the city’s published nearby intersection. Other pins approximate the operator-published locality, county, parish, island or street area; their exact parcel and entrance coordinates have not been verified. Meitner spans Gray and Roberts Counties; its marker represents Gray County, not the precise facility site.

Existing Abilene Stargate and Wisconsin Fairwater pins were identified during duplicate research and were not recreated. Equinix Lagos LG3 was excluded from operational coverage: the general expansion page had a past opening window, while a newer August operator blog still described it as scheduled to open later in 2026. KL2 and Microsoft Vaasa were researched but not included in this selection. Their development announcements alone should not be used to classify them as merely planned when annual filings or later site-preparation updates show more advanced work.

This is a sourced selection rather than an exhaustive global inventory. Status tags represent the public evidence reviewed on October 10, 2026 and need rechecking as projects progress. No pin photos or thumbnails were added to Git.
