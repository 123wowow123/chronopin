Audited all 282 live restaurant-tagged pins, including the 19 city guides and historical restaurant pins. Of 87 pins without images, all 87 now have reviewed local assets attached in the database and catalog. Existing images were preserved. All Columbus listings have images.

The final two openings now have qualified, verified images:

- **Cha Chin (Chicago, pin 6473):** Anthony Tahlier’s photo published by Chicago Food Magazine shows the exact future premises at 407 N Clark Street as Bodega in 2023. The guide and pin explicitly label it “Future premises · Bodega interior in 2023.” Eater’s opening report and the city’s October 5 liquor application match Cha Chin to that address. This is not a photo of Cha Chin’s new interior; replace it when the operator publishes current imagery.
- **Pearl Diver (Seattle, pin 6542):** The operator’s public Instagram post of August 18 identifies chef Ash Stockham and front-of-house partner Lauren. The guide and pin label this as the opening team before opening. CHS links this official account to the announced Broadway restaurant. Replace with venue or menu photography when available.
