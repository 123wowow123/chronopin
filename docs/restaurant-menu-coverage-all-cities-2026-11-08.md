### Credit correction for Japan photos (November 9)

A review of the saved Japan batches found 868 photos credited "(official website)" whose source page was a listing or press site, not the restaurant's own site (850 Hot Pepper Gourmet listings, 8 PR TIMES releases, 8 TableCheck pages, 2 ARNE media articles, 1 TV Aichi campaign page). `scripts/restaurants/fixImageCredits.py` rewrote their credit and caption to name the true source ("Photo: <venue> (via Hot Pepper Gourmet listing)" and so on), revision-checked with full readbacks; photos and all other fields were untouched. Future scans must set the credit from the source host, not assume "official website". Google Maps and delivery-platform pictures were not attempted: Hot Pepper listings were already scanned, and Maps/Tabelog photos need per-dish identity checks and carry bot-check and rights risks.

### Tabelog and Google Maps trials (November 9)

**Tabelog (owner asked for it):** scripted requests get 403, but ordinary page loads in the owner's Chrome work, so menu pages were read by navigating one page at a time and extracting the labelled dish photos from the page. Of the 9 Fukuoka/Tokyo/Nagoya venues with a Tabelog menu link, three had labelled photos whose English names matched saved items: THE CONTINENTAL ROYAL & Goh (21), Pasta Sakaba Pokiito (27) and GohGan (3). 51 photos were saved (originals downloaded from Tabelog's image host with a Tabelog referrer, each credited "via Tabelog menu listing" and linked to the menu page). Skipped: pages whose photos carry no dish name (Niku to Tamago's photo tab), LATURE (one generic cuisine photo), Taishu Sakaba French Man (none), drink-only pages (none). The saved item names matched Tabelog's English names exactly, so use `/en/` pages for matching. Tabelog's per-item photos are posted by the shop or reviewers; the owner's rule is to credit the source, which the credit does.

**Google Maps:** one search was run in the owner's Chrome for the largest unphotographed venue (Uotto Fish Man, Sapporo Station). Maps found no match for the venue name, and its photos are uncaptioned user uploads. Assigning them to saved dishes would mean inferring a dish from an unlabelled photo, which the guide forbids, and doing it for roughly 21,000 unphotographed items is not feasible. No Google photos were saved; a Maps place with a labelled Menu or Popular dishes section could be used case by case.
