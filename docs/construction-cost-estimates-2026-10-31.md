## Lessons

- Research agents read pages through a summarizing fetcher, so quotes are not verbatim. Check every quote against the raw page (`.tmp/check_quote.py` approach: curl, strip tags, normalise, substring) before saving.
- Open sources rarely give a per-seat, per-bed or per-key range. Stadium and hotel estimates need a purchased benchmark (HVS Hotel Development Cost Survey, Arcadis or Turner & Townsend tables) or two comparables with their own sourced cost and size.
- 2235: the two award figures (S$1,814,961,863 vs "1,395 Billion") belong to one tender; the primary GeBIZ/MOH notice was unreadable. Replace the midpoint with the base-offer figure if a primary record is found.
