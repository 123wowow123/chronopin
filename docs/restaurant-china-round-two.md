# China restaurant guides: second additional round

Checked October 10, 2026. Ten established restaurants, one each in Kunming, Foshan, Dongguan, Jinan, Hefei, Xiamen, Ningbo, Dalian, Shenyang and Harbin. The [reviewed snapshot](restaurant-china-round-two.json) contains 59 named dishes and 18 credited images. Six Minxin dishes include published CNY listing prices; the other 53 explicitly show “Price not published.” Chronopin selection is editorial, not a star rating. Guide dates are research dates, not opening dates.

Menus, details and tiles are saved through the local restaurant-content API; media are linked through the local pin/media APIs. The snapshot records sources and can be reviewed independently. No production database import, deployment, Git push or restart is authorized by this round. Live edits continue to use database management APIs rather than a deployment.

## Coverage and media limits

All ten additions go into Top restaurants. Research checked operator pages, branch-specific listings, local reporting, linked galleries, menu images, PDFs and promotion leads. No verified forthcoming opening, qualifying recent opening, or active/upcoming dated special was established for these ten branches. Existing guides retain their researched openings/offers; empty tabs remain hidden and links fall back to a populated section. No opening dates, discounts or special-menu eligibility are inferred from regular dishes. Full menus, most current prices and authenticated operator ordering/promotional pages remain gaps.

Every image is stored in Azure Blob Storage with original URL, source page, attribution and a caption. Each public blob was downloaded, SHA-256 checked and decoded before referencing it. Source images and generated WebP derivatives stay in the ignored `.scrape/china-restaurants-round-two` folder, never in Git. Gallery visitor photographs do not independently verify current menu items. Kunming, Hefei and Xiamen use approximate hotel-property map positions; Shenyang uses a disclosed approximate Zhongshan Road geocode. Trip.com does not document the coordinate datum for Cui He; its map location is approximate. The seven Amap locations were converted from GCJ-02 to WGS84. Country and city navigation order remains west to east.
