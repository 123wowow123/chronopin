# More local restaurants in ten Chinese cities

Checked October 10, 2026. [Import snapshot](restaurant-china-more-restaurants.json).

Ten additional established restaurants, one in each of Kunming, Foshan, Dongguan, Jinan, Hefei, Xiamen, Ningbo, Dalian, Shenyang and Harbin. These expand the existing guides with local cooking and snack shops. All are editorial **Top restaurants** selections; no rating, opening date or award has been invented. October 10 on each guide pin is its research date, not the restaurant's opening date. Country/city navigation remains ordered by longitude.

The snapshot contains **58 named branch dishes**, one separately described merchant gift offer, and **22 credited photographs**. Accessible sources did not provide reliable current individual dish prices. Items say **Price not published**; estimated per-person spending, old reviews and another branch's prices are not menu prices. These are selections, not full menus. Regular dishes are never assumed to participate in an offer.

Menus, visit details, landing tiles and the one verified offer are saved through the local database management APIs. This file and JSON preserve research evidence; they are not runtime catalogs. No production deployment or production database import is authorized by this addition request.

No verified upcoming or recent opening was recovered for these ten established branches. Existing city Coming soon, Just opened and Specials content remains available where eligible; empty sections remain hidden with a populated-view fallback.

The branch listing's daily 09:30–21:30 hours provide the provisional service window; visitors must confirm redemption with the branch. The offer menu is separate from normal dishes. No gift is invented, assigned a monetary value or labelled as a free meal. Other branches' ice-dessert gifts and hours are not borrowed. The Specials card remains eligible outside the service window and shows its next local start; it expires after the published campaign end.

Remaining gaps: current itemised prices/full menus for all ten restaurants; operator-owned branch gallery access where guest photos were used; precise entrance coordinates for Hao Ge Xian Sao; Fuzhaolou's specific gift and additional redemption terms.
