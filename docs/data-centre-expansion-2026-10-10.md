## Dates, locations and conflicting evidence

Timeline dates identify the stated milestone: opening, groundbreaking, announcement, site-preparation update, or forecast window. An operational site's dated milestone can be its initial investment or construction start, rather than its opening. These distinctions appear in the card and detailed summary. Unknown opening dates are not invented.

Year-only dates use January 1; month-only dates use the first of the month; seasonal and quarterly dates use an explicitly qualified representative day. Those dates are marked estimated, with reasoning that the day is a placeholder. Exact dated operator or government announcements are marked confirmed. Different conventions used by the first 15 pins remain documented in their original notes.

Most markers approximate a published city, county, parish, district or campus area. Geocoding matches were reviewed, but coordinates are not surveyed entrances. Street-based markers also disclose their precision. Microsoft Ginger East and Ginger West are separate named projects, but their exact parcels remain unverified, so city-level markers can coincide. Google's Kansas City proposal is a city-level pin for two proposed facilities whose separate parcels have not been established.

Equinix JH2's dated topping-out release forecasts Q3 2027, while its current expansion timetable lists Q1 2027. Both forecasts are retained; neither proves opening. The Iskandar Puteri locality is inferred from an indexed Equinix JH2 recruitment listing; the expired direct listing returns 404, and no precise parcel is claimed. LG3 remains under construction because a newer August 2026 operator update still describes opening later in 2026, superseding earlier elapsed forecasts. Microsoft San Bovio remains planned because its update forecasts a construction start without confirming that it occurred.

Meta Rosemount has conflicting evidence: its operator sheet still describes it as forthcoming, while Epoch AI estimates June 2026 operation by extrapolating three months of commissioning from March satellite imagery. Its construction/commissioning classification is explicitly provisional; the estimate is included as a lower-confidence reference and does not establish an actual opening. Some Singapore directories similarly label Meta's facility operational from older announcements or schedules, but the linked operator sheet still uses future tense and no definitive opening announcement was found. These sites need direct commissioning confirmation.
