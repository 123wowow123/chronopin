# Japan restaurant guides

Collection researched October 9, 2026. Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka use the shared
restaurant guide, `Asia/Tokyo`, kilometre distances and west-to-east navigation
(Osaka, Kyoto, Tokyo). Countries sort by their westernmost supported city.

All guides support San Diego's four sections: Coming soon, Just opened, Top
restaurants and Specials. Keep Specials visible for verified active or upcoming
offers regardless of review coverage or whether service has started. Upcoming
cards show the next local start time; expired offers are excluded. Show a tab
only when its section has eligible content. Links to empty sections fall back to
a populated section. Research every section thoroughly before accepting a gap;
record checked sources and remaining gaps here. Never invent dates, menus,
discounts or review scores to expose a tab.
